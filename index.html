Principle

Height is not a trait. It is a law.

In Danavpur, stature shapes culture, society and myth. The world is read from the ground upward: height, elevation, cloud, monument, civilisation, mythology, realm, cosmos.

Danavpur is an original fictional universe. This site is its living archive, and new records will be added as the mythology grows.
